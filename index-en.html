02 — The origin
ὄρνιςórnis · «bird» — root of ornithology

ORNITHEA was born from wonder: the contemplation of birds as a symbol of freedom, serenity and harmony with the living world.

Through photography and the observation of their habitats, an acute awareness emerges of how fragile these ecosystems are. The fast fashion industry has normalised the disconnection between dressing, life and nature. ORNITHEA responds with a fashion that reconnects people to the world through respect, education and conscious beauty.

We believe that true luxury is silent, honest and coherent. We don't follow fleeting trends: we honour time, quality and intention.

ORNITHEA is dressing with awareness. It's choosing less, but better.
